# SPAMPY r932 source snapshot

This archive is the maintained source baseline for future SPAMPY updates.

Included customisations:

- Requested unused factory sounds removed from both the preset menu and compiled preset tables.
- The former NOISE engine slot is the lightweight MORPH wavetable engine; the NOISE source was removed.
- MORPH has the custom factory bank, including CITY LIGHTS, RETRO GLASS, PPG BELL and WAVE STRINGS.
- Obervave factory presets no longer use random vintage pitch drift by default; EDIT > VINT re-enables it deliberately.
- Synthwave/80s preset additions: NIGHT DRIVER, VHS PAD, JUNO CHORUS, POLY COMP, DX MARIMBA, TURBO BASS, MIDNIGHT ARP, SEQ PULSE, GATE STAB and STREET ORGAN.
- JUNO CHORUS is the r930 bright, rhythmic poly variant, deliberately distinct from VHS PAD.
- SYNWAVE is an additional drum kit placed last in the factory-kit list, preserving all existing saved-project kit indices.
- GHOST CHOIR was removed as it overlapped too much with VOX PAD.
- GLACIER and STILL ORBIT were removed as they overlapped too much with CATHEDRAL HUM.
- Firmware OTA product identity: FM-1_932.

The matching installable firmware is `SPAMPY-r930-juno-chorus-bright.fwsc`.
